About Us

We turn travel ideas into real trips

TravelBloom is a travel recommendation company that helps people discover beaches, temples and cities around the world and plan their visit with confidence.

Who we are

TravelBloom started with a simple idea: choosing where to go should be the most exciting part of a trip, not the most stressful. We gather the essentials for each destination in one place: what makes it special, what the local time is right now, and which airport to fly into.

Our recommendations cover three kinds of travel: relaxing beach holidays, cultural temple visits, and city breaks across different countries. Every destination is reviewed by our team before it appears on the site.

Guidelines for team members

Every TravelBloom team member follows these instructions when working on the website and with customers.

  1. Reply within 24 hours. Answer every message from the Contact Us form within one working day.
  2. Check facts before publishing. Verify each destination's description, time zone and nearest airport from at least two reliable sources.
  3. Update the data file, not the pages. Add or change destinations in travel_recommendation_api.json, then test the search for beach, temple and country.
  4. Use the right images. Use only images we own or have permission to use, and give each one a clear description (alt text).
  5. Keep a friendly, simple tone. Write short, clear sentences that any traveller can understand.
  6. Protect customer data. Never share a customer's name, email or travel plans outside the team.
  7. Escalate problems early. Report broken links, wrong information or unhappy customers to the Team Lead the same day.
